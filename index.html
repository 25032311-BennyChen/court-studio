COURT STUDIO / CONCEPT IN PROGRESS

A personal identity.
A clear purpose.

An internship portfolio that connects my technical studies with my interest in UI/UX and visual design.

The idea

“Court” comes from my interest in badminton. “Studio” represents a space to explore ideas, make things and keep improving.

The visual direction

Forest green, ivory and a lime accent. Court lines create structure, while a BC monogram gives the portfolio a personal signature.

The build

This portfolio is built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The Home, Portfolio and About pages share a consistent visual identity, with coursework case studies and the Contact page still to come.

Current stage: developing the portfolio and adding coursework. More project examples will be added separately.
